## Outcome

The plan now defines a self-contained future deny-by-default contract for
generic action requests. It separates approval, authorization, and execution;
binds authority to immutable request fingerprints; and specifies explicit
state, expiry, idempotency, concurrency, and redacted audit behavior.

Deterministic fixture cases cover explicit authorization, approval gating,
rejection, expiry, replay, idempotency conflicts, fingerprint drift, invalid
parameters, unavailable context, and transition races without executing any
action.
