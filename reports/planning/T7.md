## Outcome

The plan defines a provider-neutral, point-in-time snapshot contract with four
explicit field states: `known`, `stale`, `unknown`, and `unsupported`. It fixes
field semantics, provenance and fixture honesty, freshness thresholds,
allowlist-first redaction, stable top-level errors, deterministic fixture cases,
acceptance checks, assumptions, bounded risks, and deferred decisions.

The contract remains planning-only. It adds no process or session inspection,
OS or hardware API access, runtime control, monitoring, persistence, transport,
UI, dependency, or application-contract change.

## Key decisions

- V1 uses one provider per immutable snapshot and does not merge sources.
- Every field is required and carries an explicit state; absence and `null` are
  invalid.
- Stale last-known values remain visible with their evidence time rather than
  becoming unknown.
- Known capability absence is distinct from provider-level lack of support.
- Fixture provenance is mandatory and machine-readable.
- Redaction fails closed, and raw payload retention is prohibited.
- Field-level uncertainty is a successful snapshot; only an unsafe or invalid
  envelope produces a stable top-level error.
