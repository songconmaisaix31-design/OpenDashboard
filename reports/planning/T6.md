## Outcome

T6 now has a self-contained, provider-neutral contract proposal for diagnosing
one normalized 5xx fixture request. It defines closed inputs and outputs,
machine-readable fixture provenance, deterministic correlation, redaction,
confidence and limitation rules, stable result and failure states, a 5xx
fixture matrix, and ten acceptance checks.

The central safety decision is that confidence measures only support for a
controlled observation statement. Every result states that causality is not
established, and missing or conflicting evidence produces a limited or
no-finding result instead of a guessed root cause.

## Scope record

Planned:

- pre-normalized fixture request, HTTP, trace, and log evidence shapes;
- exact request-ID, trace-ID, and bounded contextual correlation;
- allowlist redaction with non-echoing stable errors;
- deterministic 500, 502, 503, and 504 positive fixtures plus ambiguity,
  no-match, conflict, and sensitive-input cases;
- contract-level acceptance checks, assumptions, risks, and deferrals.

Not planned or performed:

- interception, proxying, replay, live tracing, network access, or provider
  integration;
- actions, approvals, process control, persistence, export, or UI;
- mappings to any other task model;
- application changes, dependency changes, deployment, upload, merge, or push.

## Key decisions

1. Version 1 accepts fixture mode only and rejects provider extension bags.
2. Explicit identifiers take priority; contradictory identifiers fail closed.
3. Contextual correlation requires exact method and route plus an inclusive
   ±2,000 ms window and always remains a limited finding without stronger
   evidence.
4. Input ordering cannot affect output; IDs, limitation codes, and error paths
   are sorted before return.
5. Raw payloads and arbitrary text never cross the contract. Findings use
   controlled statements, and rejection never echoes unsafe values.
6. No evidence is a valid `no_supported_finding`, not an internal error.

## Acceptance coverage

The plan specifies contract checks for schema bounds, redaction, correlation
edges, evidence permutation, confidence levels, every fixture outcome,
non-causality language, repeatability, fixture provenance, and excluded
surfaces. These checks are specifications for future implementation; there is
currently no application source or runnable contract test suite.

## Residual risks

- A trusted fixture normalizer is assumed but not designed, so its redaction
  correctness is not proven by the receipt it emits.
- Even exact correlation proves co-occurrence rather than causation.
- Fixed signals and aligned fixture clocks deliberately omit production
  ambiguity and must not be presented as live readiness.
- Reconciliation with other plans or product contracts remains a separate
  post-freeze decision.
